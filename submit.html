Submit

We read everything that comes in. We publish four issues a year, which means we take a small number of pieces and give each one a lot of room.

What we publish

Essays, reportage, fiction, poetry, photography, interviews, artwork, and field notes. We are less interested in what category a submission fits into, and more interested in the piece itself.

Send finished work or pitches. Simultaneous submissions to other publications are fine, just tell us if it gets picked up elsewhere. We respond to everything, though it can take a while.

No A.I.
No Exceptions.

We do not publish writing generated or rewritten by a language model, images generated or extended by one, or photographs altered beyond ordinary darkroom corrections (exposure, contrast, crop, dust.)

The whole reason this journal is printed and not posted is that a person made the thing and another person is holding it.

Submitting means you're confirming the work is yours. If we find otherwise, we will withdraw the piece.

Where we come from

The Void owes most of its temperament to the small journals of the fifties and sixties, specifically the ones the French existentialists ran. Sartre, Beauvoir, and Camus argued in the pages of magazines they founded and edited and paid for.

We value Kierkegaard for the insistence that the interesting questions are the uncomfortable ones, and for publishing them himself when no one else would, and Thompson for the conviction that the reporter is in the room and might as well admit it.

Stylistic recommendations

Your voice on the page matters. Take a position. Write boldly. Be in the piece if you were there. Don't be afraid to offend, and write like you have to. The Void prefers punches to petticoats and well-fought disagreement to mundane niceties.

We often publish good pieces we disagree with. We have no political affiliation.
