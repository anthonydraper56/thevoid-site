What we're
about

The Void is a quarterly literary and culture journal that exists on paper and nowhere else. We publish fiction, essays, poetry, photography, art, and interviews. Basically, anything that makes a reader put the phone down for an hour.

Nothing here gets posted, syndicated, or fed to a machine. If you want to read it, you have to physically hold it in your hands. That's the whole idea, and after two years and ten issues, I think it's catching on.
